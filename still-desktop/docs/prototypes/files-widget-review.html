Community Research Grant Agreement

Funding agreement　·　Effective 12 June 2025

This agreement sets out the terms under which the Foundation provides support to the Recipient for the approved community research program.

1. Funding and schedule

The Foundation will provide a total grant of $48,000, payable in two instalments. The first payment will be made within thirty days of signing.

The Recipient must submit the interim report by September 30, 2025. The final report and expenditure statement are due within sixty days of the project end date.

2. Eligibility and use of funds

Grant funds may only be used for direct project expenses and approved participant support. Any material change to the approved project scope requires written approval before expenses are incurred.

The Recipient is responsible for maintaining complete records and must retain receipts for a period of seven years after the final payment.
